# Wake & Bake POTIFY Price Update
## Whitepaper · public developer edition

**November 25, 2026 · Editorial production kit**

### Goal and audience

Make a short, time-stamped daily report readers can check for themselves. The immediate audience is existing community viewers and potential editorial sponsors.

### Design and implementation

An episode worksheet separates market observation, chart time, source and founder commentary. Seven slots correspond to the existing chart-led format. Attach the interval and time zone to every chart. When a market is thin, a stale last trade and a new quote can tell different stories.

### What a contributor can establish

The commands below ran in a clean test context using local files and fixtures. Separate a sourced observation from a host's interpretation. State the missing observation instead of interpolating price or volume.

### Evidence and outstanding work

No live quotes, trading signal, unapproved post copy or sponsor agreement is supplied. The next acceptance case is concrete. A reviewer can trace each chart to its source and timestamp before the episode is approved.

### Place in POTIFY

Qualify one explicitly disclosed show sponsor after audience and placement are measured. The marketing plan gives the test window and the evidence for a commercial outcome.
