# Wake & Bake POTIFY Price Update marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Existing community viewers and potential editorial sponsors. **Offer.** Make a short, time-stamped daily report readers can check for themselves.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Share a captioned sample showing how a dated observation is checked, with a link to the episode notes. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Review one episode worksheet. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 5 repeat viewers across two approved episodes. |

### Sales test

For a proposed two-episode sponsorship, request a written brief and price only after measured audience data exists. Give delivered episodes and paid invoice IDs separate evidence. Qualify one explicitly disclosed show sponsor after audience and placement are measured.

### Measurement and decision

Use `wake-and-bake-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** No live quotes, trading signal, unapproved post copy or sponsor agreement is supplied. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
